GoBench measures how well frontier language models play 9×9 Go against a calibrated ladder of KataGo opponents.

We believe that GoBench can measure progress toward AGI. We list three ways to saturate GoBench, and all three can produce progress toward AGI.

1. **Transfer from GoBench optimizations.** Researchers may develop novel architectures or learning algorithms while optimizing for GoBench, and those ideas can potentially transfer to other tasks.
2. **Lower-cost learning.** As the cost of learning a new domain decreases, an AI may learn Go with less human supervision. As the cost continues to decrease, given a sufficient budget, such an AI can attempt to learn many economically valuable domains.
3. **Improved general reasoning.** Stronger general reasoning can lead to stronger Go performance without the model having previously learned Go. If an AI’s general reasoning alone can saturate GoBench, it can likely handle much economically valuable work.
