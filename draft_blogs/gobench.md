## Why GoBench?

We believe GoBench can measure progress toward AGI. There are three ways to saturate the benchmark, each of which could advance that progress:

1. **Transfer from GoBench optimizations**

   Researchers may develop novel architectures or learning algorithms while optimizing for GoBench. Those ideas could transfer to other tasks.

2. **Lower-cost learning**

   As the cost of learning a new domain decreases, an AI may learn Go with less human supervision. Given a sufficient budget, it could then attempt to learn many economically valuable domains.

3. **Improved general reasoning**

   Stronger general reasoning could improve Go performance without the model having previously learned Go. If general reasoning alone can saturate GoBench, it could likely support much economically valuable work.
